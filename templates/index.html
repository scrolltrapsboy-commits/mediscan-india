About MediScan India

A photo is cleaned up with OpenCV (grayscale, noise reduction and edge analysis), its printed text is read with OCR, and Gemini uses the image and text to suggest a possible medicine. Results are unverified. A loose tablet with no printed marking cannot be identified from a photo.

Privacy: photos are processed in memory to read the text and are not stored. Terms: this is an informational tool and does not replace professional medical advice.
