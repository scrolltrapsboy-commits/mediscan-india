Augmentin 625 Duo Tablet

Amoxicillin and Potassium Clavulanate Tablets IP

Active Ingredients Amoxicillin (500mg) + Potassium Clavulanate (125mg)
Strength 625 mg Total
Manufacturer GlaxoSmithKline Pharma (GSK)
healing Primary Uses

Bacterial infections of the respiratory tract (bronchitis, pneumonia), ear, nose, throat, skin, and urinary tract.

info Common Side Effects

Mild nausea, loose stools, vomiting, skin rash. Usually temporary and resolves with hydration and meals.

shield Clinical Precautions

Inform your doctor if allergic to penicillin. Complete the prescribed course even if symptoms subside. Take with a meal to minimize stomach upset.
