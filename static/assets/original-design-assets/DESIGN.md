## Brand & Style

This design system embodies clinical precision, absolute trust, and effortless clarity. Designed for a high-stakes healthcare environment where users verify, identify, and compare medications, the aesthetic mirrors the meticulous simplicity of modern clinical operating suites paired with the refined restraint of Apple Health.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Patients, caregivers, and medical practitioners across India requiring instantaneous, unambiguous pill and prescription identification, verified generic-versus-branded pricing comparisons, and critical dosage guidance.
- **Emotional Response:** Immediate reassurance, scientific certainty, cognitive calm, and zero anxiety. The UI eliminates decorative visual noise in favor of pristine readability and high legibility under stressful conditions.

### Design Movement: Modern Clinical Precision
A synthesis of hyper-clean minimalism and tactile digital utility:
- **Pristine Canvas:** Pure stark whites layered over cool slate planes to signal sterile hygiene and clinical focus.
- **Architectural Structure:** Subtle hairline divides (`#E2E8F0`), micro-surfaces, and deliberate data-density tiers that make complex pharmaceutical data scannable in milliseconds.
- **Trust Elements:** Institutional-grade verification markers, official safety check indicators, clear regulatory stamps, and localized Indian Rupee currency matrices formatted for instant mental math.

## Colors

The palette establishes unassailable authority through deep clinical slates paired with active bio-teal and emerald verification tones.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#0D9488` - Bio Teal):** Active interaction states, high-priority actions, focus rings, and verified identity indicators.
- **Secondary (`#059669` - Clinical Emerald):** Savings differentials, authentic generic validation, safe drug-interaction statuses, and success states.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Diagnostic Blue):** Informational callouts, active AI processing indicators, and clinical mechanism hyperlinks.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** Grounded contrast for critical pharmacology typography, ensuring zero optical vibration against pale panels.

### Surface System
- **Base Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
- **Subsurface Panels / Secondary Cards:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50)
- **Nested Wells / Metric Fields / Disabled Surfaces:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100)
- **Structural Dividers & Outlines:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200)
- **Secondary Type / Labelling:** `#475569` (Slate 600)
- **Muted Type / Metadata:** `#64748B` (Slate 500)

### Healthcare Semantic Signals
- **Critical Alert / Contraindication:** `#E11D48` (Rose 600) on `#FFF1F2` (Rose 50) with `#FDA4AF` (Rose 300) border.
- **Precaution / Composition Divergence:** `#D97706` (Amber 600) on `#FFFBEB` (Amber 50) with `#FCD34D` (Amber 300) border.
- **Verified Authentic / Generic Parity:** `#059669` (Emerald 600) on `#ECFDF5` (Emerald 50) with `#A7F3D0` (Emerald 200) border.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a clinical dual-type pairing: **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers approachable, authoritative structural geometry for headlines and analytical titles, while **Inter** guarantees unmatched micro-legibility for chemical compounds, pharmacological dosages, contraindications, and comparative data tables.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Currency & Metric Stacking:** Always display the Indian Rupee symbol (`₹`) inline with numeric tabular figures (e.g., `₹142.50`). Never allow floating symbols separated from the value.
- **Dosage & Salt Names:** Active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs, e.g., *Paracetamol 500mg + Caffeine 30mg*) must be presented in `body-sm` using Slate 600 (`#475569`) directly beneath the primary trade brand name to prevent user misidentification.
- **Caps & Badges:** Use `label-sm` with strict uppercase casing (`text-transform: uppercase`) and wide track spacing (`0.04em`) exclusively for clinical tags, regulatory confirmations, and inventory availability indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 8pt spatial baseline maintains predictable scanning rhythms across high-density diagnostic comparisons and mobile-first pharmacy lookups.

### Grid Model
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid. `2rem` outer page margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Max-width container capped at `1280px` for readability of tabular drug monographs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout. `1.5rem` page margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Side-by-side comparison tables adapt to horizontal scroll cards with sticky leading parameters.
- **Mobile (<767px):** 4-column layout. `1rem` page margins, `1rem` gutters. Comparison columns reflow into a vertically stacked pill-selector format with pinned floating summary actions.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Component Interiors:** Dense data-wells (e.g., salt composition breakdowns) leverage `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Structural cards maintain an interior boundary of `space-lg` to prevent cognitive claustrophobia.
- **Section Stack:** High-level module breaks (e.g., AI Visual Scan Module vs. Verified Generic Equivalents List) maintain a distinct `space-xl` separation.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve the pristine, sterile aesthetic, elevation relies on subtle tonal containment and soft diffused atmospheric shadowing rather than heavy drops.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Pure `#FFFFFF` canvas. Structural division through `#E2E8F0` 1px borders.
- **Level 1 (Subsurface Cards):** `#F8FAFC` background with a 1px border of `#E2E8F0`. No shadow. Applied to secondary data tables, ingredient disclosures, and search parameter panels.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards & Scan Wells):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px border of `#E2E8F0`, underpinned by a soft diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Floating Price Action Bars):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with an elevated soft shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (AI Scanner Active Reticle):** Backdrop filter blur `blur(12px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a glowing primary halo: `0 0 0 1px #0D9488, 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.20)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level `2` (0.5rem base radius). This geometry creates precise, ergonomic corners that channel Apple Health's friendly accessibility without compromising the crisp discipline demanded by clinical data tools.

### Radius Applications
- **Base Components (`rounded` - 0.5rem / 8px):** Input fields, inline badges, clinical tag groups, buttons, drop-down selects.
- **Container Surfaces (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Primary drug profile cards, comparison side-by-side matrices, camera prescription upload dropzones.
- **Overlays & Dialogs (`rounded-xl` - 1.5rem / 24px):** Medicine detail bottom-sheets on mobile, diagnostic image preview lightboxes, alert modals.
- **Full Radius (`rounded-full`):** Status indicator dots, active AI scanning pills, step count indicators, and verified security check chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Scan / Compare):** Solid `#0D9488` with `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, 0.5rem border-radius. Subtle hover transition to `#0F766E`. Active compression scale `0.99`.
- **Secondary (Alternative Generics):** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC` with border tone `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive / Caution:** `#FFF1F2` background, `#E11D48` text, 1px solid `#FDA4AF`.

### Verified Trust Badges & Clinical Chips
- **Generic Match Status Chip:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`. Light emerald surface (`#ECFDF5`), deep emerald text (`#059669`), 1px solid `#A7F3D0`. Includes a 12px trailing checkmark icon.
- **Formulation Tag:** `rounded` (4px), padding `2px 8px`, `#F1F5F9` background, `#475569` text, `label-sm` font. Used for "Schedule H", "Prescription Required", or "Strip of 10 Tablets".

### Medicine Comparison Cards
- **Structure:** Dual or triple-column layout on desktop. Header carries the brand name (`headline-sm`), followed by active chemical salt composition (`body-sm`), followed by an isolated **Price Delta Box**.
- **Price Delta Box:** Embedded `#F8FAFC` well with a `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`. Highlights the branded price struck through in Slate 400 (`₹180.00`) alongside the verified generic price in Bold Emerald 600 (`₹32.50`) and an auto-calculated pill: `"Save 82%"`.

### Input & Search Fields
- **Search Engine Field:** Large `48px` height input, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, pure `#FFFFFF` background with embedded leading clinical search icon and trailing camera icon for AI photo-scan trigger. Focus state shifts border directly to `#0D9488` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)` optical ring.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** `20px x 20px` square (`rounded` 4px) for checkboxes, circular for radios. Inactive border is 1.5px `#CBD5E1`. Active state fills `#0D9488` with sharp white tick icon.

### AI Prescription Upload Dropzone
- **Interactive Container:** `rounded-lg` bounding area with a dashed 1.5px border in `#0D9488` on a `#F0FDFA` (Teal 50) soft wash. Features real-time optical scanning guide lines that sweep across uploaded imagery during verification analysis.
